About

Context on where this work comes from.

I'm an undergraduate in Computer Science Engineering and Artificial Intelligence at VIT Bhopal University, currently entering my third year. My research centers on applying computer vision and deep learning to problems with tangible real-world stakes: detecting structural cracks before they become safety hazards, making Braille literacy accessible through ordinary smartphones, and building smarter surveillance systems using CNNs.

Alongside coursework, I've co-invented a patented structural crack detection system built on multimodal sensor fusion and AI, and hold a granted industrial design registration for the physical device. I'm also a recipient of the Raman Research Award at VIT Bhopal, awarded for research contribution as a co-author.

Current Work

In progress

Smart CCTV Surveillance Application

A real-time surveillance dashboard built with FastAPI and OpenCV: live video is streamed to the browser over WebSockets, motion events trigger automatic clip recording with thumbnail previews, and the interface supports light/dark viewing modes. CNN-based event classification is the next milestone, moving the system from motion-triggered recording to learned anomaly detection.
